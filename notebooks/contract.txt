EDA Insight 1:

Contract type has a strong relationship with customer churn.

Month-to-month customers have the highest observed churn rate
(42.71%), while two-year customers have the lowest (2.83%).


EDA Insight 2:

Tenure has a strong relationship with customer churn.

Customers with 0–12 months of tenure have a 47.44% churn
rate, compared with only 9.51% for customers with 49–72
months of tenure.

EDA Insight 3:

Monthly charges are associated with customer churn.

The Low charge group has an 11.24% churn rate,
while the Medium-High group has the highest rate at 37.51%.
The High group has a slightly lower churn rate of 32.88%.

EDA Insight 4:

Internet service is strongly associated with customer churn.

Fiber-optic customers have the highest observed churn rate
at 41.89%, compared with 18.96% for DSL customers and
7.40% for customers without internet service.

EDA Insight 5:

Payment method is strongly associated with customer churn.

Electronic-check customers have the highest observed churn
rate at 45.29%, while credit-card automatic customers have
the lowest at 15.24%.

EDA Insight 6:

Tech Support is strongly associated with customer churn.

Customers without Tech Support have a 41.64% churn rate,
while customers with Tech Support have a 15.17% churn rate.
